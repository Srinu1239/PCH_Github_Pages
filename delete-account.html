How to delete your account

You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the PG Connect Hub app:

  1. Open the App and Log In.
  2. Open the side menu (Drawer) and go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom to find the Delete Account section.
  4. Follow the prompts to confirm deletion.

Don't have the app installed?

If you no longer have the app installed and wish to request account and data deletion, please send an email to our support team. Include the email address associated with your account.

Request Deletion via Email

Note: In accordance with our security policy, account deletion is subject to verification that all platform obligations (e.g., checkout completion, order fulfillment, or active listings) have been resolved. Deletion typically results in the permanent removal of your profile, documents, and transaction history from our active servers.
